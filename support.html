Adding the widget

On the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area and choose Lock&Learn. On the Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area, tap the plus button in the corner, search for Lock&Learn, and add the size you like.

Free trial and purchases

Some levels and packs unlock with an optional purchase. The yearly plan starts with a free trial, and nothing is charged until the trial ends. A one-time lifetime option is also available. All payments are handled by Apple through your App Store account.

Managing or canceling a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, and choose Lock&Learn. From there you can change or cancel the plan at any time. Canceling stops the next renewal and keeps your access until the current period ends.

Restoring a purchase

Using a new device, or reinstalled the app? Open the app, go to the unlock screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Your previous purchase will be reactivated on the same Apple account.

Your privacy
