answered

How do I put Spanish flashcards on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Install Lock&Learn Spanish, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, and choose Lock&Learn Spanish. A Spanish word or expression will wait there every time your screen wakes. There is a Home Screen widget too.

What kind of Spanish does Lock&Learn Spanish teach?

Standard Spanish, the kind taught in language schools. What you learn is understood across Spain and Latin America.

Which levels does Lock&Learn Spanish cover?

Every CEFR level, from A1 to C2. Vocabulary is organized by level, so you can start where you are and let the widget carry you up. A1 is free.

Is Lock&Learn Spanish free?

Yes. The app is free on iOS: A1 is free, every other level opens with a free preview, and another word unlocks daily. Lock&Learn+ is an optional upgrade that opens every level at once, offered as a yearly plan with a free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does it teach whole expressions or just single words?

Both. Spanish runs on collocations and set phrases, so they appear as cards alongside single words, each one confirmed against the dictionary.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking. Streaks, saves and progress live on your phone and nowhere else, and notifications are opt-in. More in the Privacy Policy or on the Support page.
