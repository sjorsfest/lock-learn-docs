Privacy Policy

Lock&Learn · Last updated 4 July 2026

Lock&Learn is built so that your data stays yours. The short version: we do not collect anything.

No data collection

Lock&Learn has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of its own. Everything the app knows, such as which topics you enabled, which facts you saved, and your streak, is stored on your device and shared only with the app's own widgets through Apple's App Group storage. It never leaves your phone.

Notifications

If you turn on fact notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved.

Topic requests

If you choose to request a new topic, the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled draft. Sending it is up to you, and the email arrives like any ordinary email you would write yourself.

Purchases

If Lock&Learn offers optional purchases in the future, payment is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details.

Children

Lock&Learn does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
