05 · Questions

Asked and
answered

How do I put Arabic flashcards on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Install Lock&Learn Arabic, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, and choose Lock&Learn Arabic. An Arabic word with its romanization will wait there every time your screen wakes. There is a Home Screen widget too.

What kind of Arabic does Lock&Learn Arabic teach?

Modern Standard Arabic, the shared written language of news, books and formal speech across the Arab world. Vocabulary is graded by CEFR level, so what you learn reads the same from Rabat to Baghdad.

Which levels does Lock&Learn Arabic cover?

Every CEFR level, from A1 to C2. Vocabulary is organized by level, so you can start where you are and let the widget carry you up. A1 is free.

Is Lock&Learn Arabic free?

Yes. The app is free on iOS: A1 is free, every other level opens with a free preview, and another word unlocks daily. Lock&Learn+ is an optional upgrade that opens every level at once, offered as a yearly plan with a free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I use it before I can read Arabic script?

Yes. Every word appears fully vocalized, with all its vowel marks, and a clear romanization sits right under it on the question side. You can sound out every card from day one, and the script grows familiar with every glance.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking. Streaks, saves and progress live on your phone and nowhere else, and notifications are opt-in. More in the Privacy Policy or on the Support page.
