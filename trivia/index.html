05 · Questions

Asked and
answered

How do I add the trivia widget to my iPhone Lock Screen?

Install Lock&Learn, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, and choose Lock&Learn. A question will wait there every time your screen wakes. There is a Home Screen widget too: touch and hold an empty area, tap the plus button, and search for Lock&Learn.

Is Lock&Learn free?

Yes. The app is free on iOS: every topic opens with a free preview and another question unlocks daily. Lock&Learn+ is an optional upgrade that opens every topic at once, offered as a yearly plan with a free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase.

What trivia topics are included?

Space, History, Human Body, Ocean, Psychology, Food, Animals, Planet Earth, Science, Sports, Arts and Music, and Inventions. You switch on only the topics you want, and new questions land every month.

Are the answers verified?

Every question ships with the fact behind the answer, checked against reliable sources before it reaches your widget, and the reveal names where it came from.

Can I read facts without the quiz?

Yes. One tap in settings flips every widget from questions to plain facts, so each glance becomes a quiet discovery instead of a quiz.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking. Streaks, saves and progress live on your phone and nowhere else, and notifications are opt-in. More in the Privacy Policy or on the Support page.
