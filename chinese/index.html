05 · Questions

Asked and
answered

How do I put Chinese flashcards on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Install Lock&Learn Chinese, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, and choose Lock&Learn Chinese. An HSK word with its pinyin will wait there every time your screen wakes. There is a Home Screen widget too.

Which HSK levels does Lock&Learn Chinese cover?

Every level of the official syllabus, from HSK 1 to HSK 6. Vocabulary is organized by level, so you can start where you are and let the widget carry you up. HSK 1 is free.

Is Lock&Learn Chinese free?

Yes. The app is free on iOS: HSK 1 is free, every other level opens with a free preview, and another word unlocks daily. Lock&Learn+ is an optional upgrade that opens the whole syllabus at once, offered as a yearly plan with a free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does it teach pronunciation?

Pinyin with full tone marks appears on the widget and everywhere in the app, so you can say each word out loud before the meaning is revealed. Pronunciation is never a guess.

How is this different from a regular flashcard app?

There is no session to sit down for. The word waits on the screen you already look at dozens of times a day, you recall it before you tap, and the meaning arrives with a real example sentence. Learning happens in the seconds you were going to spend anyway.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking. Streaks, saves and progress live on your phone and nowhere else, and notifications are opt-in. More in the Privacy Policy or on the Support page.
